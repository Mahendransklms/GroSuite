HAVE QUESTIONS?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about GroSuite's billing, trial, and implementation.

How does the 15-day free trial work?
You get instant access to our fully-featured Professional plan for 15 days without entering any credit card details. You can import employee data, test attendance tracking, run mock payroll calculations, and invite team members. At the end of the trial, you can select the plan that fits your exact needs.
Can we switch between Monthly and Annual billing later?
Yes, you can upgrade from monthly to annual billing at any point from your admin billing portal to enjoy the 20% discount. If you choose to downgrade from annual to monthly, the change will apply at the end of your prepaid annual cycle.
Which biometric hardware models are supported?
GroSuite supports leading biometric manufacturers including ZKTeco, eSSL, Realtime, Matrix, and Hikvision devices via our cloud IoT gateway agent. If you already have existing biometric devices installed, our engineering team can configure the sync during onboarding without requiring new hardware.
Are taxes (GST) included in the published prices?
The prices shown above exclude applicable local taxes (e.g., 18% GST for Indian entities). GST-compliant tax invoices with input tax credit (ITC) are automatically generated and emailed to your billing contact upon every transaction.
What happens if our team size changes mid-month?
GroSuite calculates charges based on active employees in your system. When you onboard new employees during a billing period, charges are prorated for the remainder of that billing cycle. Inactive or offboarded employees are automatically excluded from subsequent billing runs.
Can we deploy GroSuite on our own private cloud or local server?
Yes! For organizations with strict compliance, government mandates, or high-security data isolation requirements, our Custom Architecture plan supports on-premise installation, private AWS/Azure VPC deployment, and air-gapped server configurations.
